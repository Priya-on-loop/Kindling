ABOUT KINDLING

Discover what
pulls you forward.

Kindling is designed for people who know they want to explore, but aren't sure where that exploration should lead.

01 THE PROBLEM

What if your interests aren't something you can simply list?

Choosing a career often begins with questions like "What are you interested in?" or "What are you good at?"

But discovering what genuinely interests you isn't always straightforward. Sometimes you understand an interest only after spending time exploring something, returning to it, or asking deeper questions.

02 OUR APPROACH

We pay attention to what you explore.

Kindling looks beyond a single questionnaire. It observes patterns in how you interact with different areas of exploration.

01

Explore

Try different topics, activities and questions without being pushed toward one predetermined path.

02

Observe

Kindling looks at behavioral signals such as what you spend time on and what you choose to explore further.

03

Reflect

Those patterns become suggestions you can inspect, question and change.

HUMAN IN THE LOOP

Kindling doesn't decide for you.

The patterns Kindling identifies are not final answers. You remain in control of your profile and can accept, edit or reject what the system suggests.
